# Whisper Phone

The pitch in your ear. A single web page — no build step, no server, no
accounts. Open `index.html` in a browser, or visit the live page:

https://intoxicateddogwalker.github.io/whisper-phone/

## What it does

- **Ear Pitch** — a pure high tone (14–19.5 kHz), steady or pulsing. At
  these pitches the usual between-ear time and loudness differences nearly
  vanish, so the tone feels like it is ringing inside your ear rather than
  coming from the phone.
- **Binaural beat (earbuds)** — each ear gets its own pitch a few Hz apart.
  No beat exists in the air; your brain builds it, and you hear it in the
  middle of your head. This needs earbuds — on the phone speaker both ears
  hear both tones mixed together, so it does not work.
- **Spoken whisper** — the phone's own voice reads your words quietly, a
  little higher and slower than normal, with a soft breath sound mixed
  underneath.
- **Frequency test** — "Can you hear it?" checks which pitches you can hear.

## Honest limit

A phone speaker cannot make a true silent ultrasound beam — that needs
special hardware. This is the closest a phone gets: a pure high pitch that
feels like it is in your ear, and a beat your brain makes itself. It is
audible sound only. It never sends inaudible commands to anyone else's
device.

## Run it

No install. Open `index.html`, or use the live link above. Everything runs
in the page; the page loads nothing from anywhere else.
