Can you hear it? — frequency test

Tap a frequency. It plays a short tone. Most adults stop hearing somewhere around 16–18 kHz — younger ears hear higher.

How it works

The whisper: your phone's own voice reads your words quietly, a little higher and slower than normal, with a soft breath sound mixed underneath — like someone whispering right next to you.

The shimmer: a very high tone (12–19 kHz) plays along with the words. Most adults can't hear that high, so to them the whisper seems to come out of nowhere. Younger people will hear a faint ring.

Honest limit: a phone speaker can't make a true silent ultrasound beam — that needs special hardware. This is the closest a phone gets: a soft whisper plus a high tone riding along with it. Turn your volume up if you can't hear the words; the shimmer is the part that stays nearly silent.
